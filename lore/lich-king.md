# The Lich King

## The artificer

The Lich King began as a mortal artificer. He wanted immortality for himself and dominion over others. His skill gave him the means to obtain both. He designed and forged the Black Reliquary, the Crown of Voices and the Rime Scythe himself. He had already made himself a crown before he had a kingdom.

Taking up the completed Scythe was the last act of his mortal life. He knew what it would cost. His body died, the Reliquary held his soul, and he rose as the Lich King. He chose his title and took his dominion by conquest. His mortal name, birthplace and first conquest remain open.

## Appearance

The supplied portrait establishes his appearance. He is tall and broad-shouldered, with an exposed, angular grey face, swept-back silver-white hair and luminous blue eyes. The pointed metal crown follows his brow. A thick pale fur mantle lies over dark plate worked with silver ridges and sweeping points. Long strips of worn blue cloth hang from his waist and move about his legs. His gauntlets and greaves carry the same silver edging as the breastplate.

The Rime Scythe stands taller than its wielder. The dark haft ends in a metal spike. Open silver metalwork joins the shaft to an enormous curved blade, dark along the back with a cold blue cutting edge. The portrait supplies no exact dimensions. Its moon, snow and distant fortress are visual context. The older closed-helm description is superseded by the visible face and crown.

## The three artifacts

| Artifact | Established function |
| --- | --- |
| Black Reliquary | Holds the King's soul. His deliberate transformation is the only death he has experienced. The lowest vault within the Winter Seat, above its runners, is the proposed location. |
| Rime Scythe | Raises existing remains in his presence. He can also cut a Royal Seal into a site he physically occupies and later raise remains there from afar. |
| Crown of Voices | Carries his orders to the raised dead and lets him speak through them. Ordinary dead follow simple orders. Captains retain the experience and judgment to command troops in his service. |

### Royal Seals

The King chooses sites worth using again. Once he has taken a town, he can raise its dead on the spot. By sealing its burial vault, he can also claim bodies carried there after his departure. The corpses must be at the sealed site when he calls them. A death elsewhere in the town supplies no soldier by itself. Destroying the seal cuts off further remote raisings there.

Every seal dates from his reign and establishes that he reached that spot in person. A seal in a recovered human town belongs to the history of its occupation. An old cemetery in a town he has never reached is simply a cemetery. Before introducing a seal, establish when he had access and what made the site worth holding.

### Soldiers and captains

The Scythe uses what remains of the body. An intact skeleton can march. A shattered spine stays broken. Scattered fragments furnish little usable material. A recently fallen regiment offers sound bodies, equipment and officers who know their trade.

A captain can change formation, use another crossing or exploit a weakness in a wall. The Crown binds his will to the King. A captain who once defended a fortress can lead the dead through its sally port. Personal affection cannot divert his orders. Ordinary dead may repeat familiar movements from life, such as steadying a boat as someone boards. The fate of their souls remains open.

## The Winter Seat and the invasion

The Winter Seat stands on runners on the northern continental ice cap. The King sets the glacier flowing beneath it. Its progress is measured in weeks. Winter crossings can carry an army across a wetland or gulf. A thaw can cut the same army off while its captain continues campaigning.

The King established his northern dominion over centuries. The eight-year chronology begins with his invasion of Vardane. His artifacts worked at his transformation, before the centuries of refinement. Reports of towns falling daily describe the current offensive. His body has never been destroyed in battle. A first battlefield defeat remains an event for the story to reach.
