# The living world

## The war

A family near the ice may have fled three homes while a proprietor far to the south still expects his child to inherit the estate. Distance, intact roads, stored food and the timing of reports determine how people experience the same invasion. A regiment can win a siege after the town has eaten its draft oxen and lost the seed for the next harvest.

## Adventurers' guilds

Each house elects or appoints its own master according to house tradition, chooses a crest and supports named teams. Members take contracts from a public board. A missing person's family may ring the rescue bell regardless of its means. Answering that bell is the obligation that earns a house its crest. Several simultaneous calls can stretch a frontier house beyond its strength.

Rank belongs to the individual. A team can include a Copper recruit under a more senior leader.

| Rank | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Copper | Work under a sponsor, learn routes, stand watch and help bring casualties home. |
| Bronze | Take routine searches, escorts and local monster contracts independently. |
| Iron | Lead beyond reliable patrol routes and decide when to abandon a contract to save lives. |
| Steel | Coordinate several teams on hunts, evacuations and expeditions into fallen territory. |
| Silver | Direct operations across houses and commit pooled resources. |
| Mithril | Exercise independent command during a regional catastrophe. Few living adventurers hold this rank. |

## Material available for later development

The earlier map places Vardane and Cyranne on Avarra, Mereth across the southern straits and Tirren across the western sea. It proposes the Holds of Kharad, the Serevan League, Amareth and the Talren Compact. Their detailed borders, governments, policies and economies are working material. Rillcross and the Open Road Charter are also proposals.

The draft census counted 2,106 inhabited towns in Vardane, of which 1,391 still answered. If these working figures are adopted, the difference of 715 counts towns that ceased reporting. It includes evacuated and isolated communities as well as destroyed towns.

Develop the peoples by race or species first. The author wants orcs in the classic martial fantasy tradition. The earlier locality-first cultures and their accompanying religions were set aside. New societies, architecture, faiths and hierarchies remain to be developed. The protagonist and starting city are open.
