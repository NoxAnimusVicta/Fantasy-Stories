## Decisions that shaped this version

- The Lich King was a mortal artificer who knowingly chose death, immortality and dominion. His first conquest remains open. Play him as evil from the outset. His body has yet to fall in battle.
- The Reliquary houses his soul, the Scythe raises the dead and the Crown commands them. He makes Royal Seals in person after his transformation. A corpse must be within a surviving seal for a remote raising. Captains exercise tactical judgment wholly in his service. Damaged remains stay damaged. Raised mages retain knowledge and require a triggered device to use stored spellwork. They cannot cast fresh living spells.
- The supplied portrait governs his appearance. Use the exposed grey face, white hair, metal crown, pale fur, dark silver-edged armour, blue cloth and immense blue-edged Scythe. The portrait accompanies the portable handover.
- Centuries of northern conquest precede the invasion of Vardane. The fall of the Narrows opens the road south. Dunmere's seal is broken in year four. The causeways fall by year eight. Reports of towns falling daily belong to this offensive.
- Develop cultures by species. Orcs travel in nomadic clans ruled by strength. Familiar fantasy archetypes suit the setting. The war, the guilds and the wilderness support stories of their own.
- Treat a consistency repair and a creative addition as distinct. Document a correction in its authoritative file. Leave player choices to the player. Planning advances no fictional time.
